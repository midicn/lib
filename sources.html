收录原则

  • 许可硬门槛：只收明确允许再分发的内容
  • 分级标注：C1 可商用 / C2 不可商用 / C3 仅供研究学习，逐曲写入数据
  • 宁缺毋滥：许可不明或禁止再分发的源，无论规模一律不收
  • 可复核：全部判定依据存档，可查阅
  • 可下架：权利人如有异议，7 个工作日内响应

Our Principles

  • Redistribution required — no permission, no inclusion
  • Tiered labeling — C1 / C2 / C3 written per track
  • Better safe than sorry — unclear or restrictive licenses are excluded regardless of scale
  • Auditable — every decision archived and reviewable
  • Takedown-ready — rights-holder notices answered within 7 business days
