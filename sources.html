关于「中国民间歌曲集成」（C3 研究/学习）

来源：GitHub 公开数据集 github.com/m-july/Anthology-of-Chinese-Folk-Songs （论文《The Renaissance of Expert Systems: Optical Recognition of Printed Chinese Jianpu Musical Scores with Lyrics》，arXiv:2512.14758 的数据集）， 内容为《中国民间歌曲集成》14 卷的光学识别（OMR）结果，原始格式为 MIDI 与 MusicXML。

我们的判断：

  • 民歌旋律本身属传统民间文学艺术，作者不可考、流传久远，作为音乐作品通常已进入公有领域
  • 但本数据是依据《中国民间歌曲集成》的记谱／整理稿识别而来，该整理与汇编成果受版权保护——上游作者亦声明原始扫描图属受版权作品、不公开
  • 因此我们不作商用授权：这批曲目归入 C3 研究/学习，限研究、教学、学习用途，禁止商业使用与公开表演

使用要求：必须标注数据来源为「中国民间歌曲集成（ Anthology of Chinese Folk Songs，OMR 数据集，github.com/m-july/Anthology-of-Chinese-Folk-Songs）」； catalog 中该批曲目的许可字段为 TRADITIONAL-STUDY。若你计划商用，请自行向底本权利人取得授权。

数据质量：上游说明该数据集尚未完全清洗，小节线可能需要再校正；陕西卷（二）部分唱词字符缺失，建议谨慎使用。

如权利方认为不宜收录，请通过项目 Issues 告知，我们将在 7 个工作日内下架该批数据。

另：Cyber Hymnal™（10,945 首，C1 可商用）——基督教赞美诗曲调集（The Cyber Hymnal™, hymntime.com/tch，收录 1500–1945 年间曲调，4 声部和声）。站点政策原文：「Material on our site which does not have a copyright notice is … in the public domain. You may post our public domain MIDI files … on another Web site, provided you attribute them to the Cyber Hymnal™」。即署名即可再分发。我们只收录 MIDI 文件内嵌元数据中标注 Public Domain 的曲调（11,245/12,885），排除标注版权方者（1,617 个）；使用时需标注来源为 The Cyber Hymnal™（hymntime.com/tch）。

另：GiantMIDI-Piano（10,112 首，C1 可商用）——字节跳动发布、以高精度钢琴转谱系统从演奏录音转录的古典钢琴独奏集（2,704 位作曲家，1,237 小时）。许可 CC BY 4.0（仓库 README 明示；其 disclaimer 仅为标准免责条款，不含使用限制），因此归入 C1 可商用，使用时需署名数据集与论文：Kong et al., "GiantMIDI-Piano", arXiv:2010.07061 · github.com/bytedance/GiantMIDI-Piano。

About the Chinese Folk Songs Anthology (C3 / Study)

Source: the public GitHub dataset github.com/m-july/Anthology-of-Chinese-Folk-Songs — the dataset accompanying "The Renaissance of Expert Systems: Optical Recognition of Printed Chinese Jianpu Musical Scores with Lyrics" (arXiv:2512.14758). It contains OMR output for 14 volumes of the Anthology of Chinese Folk Songs, in MIDI and MusicXML.

Our assessment:

  • The melodies themselves are traditional folk music — anonymous, transmitted over generations, and generally in the public domain as musical works
  • However, these files were recognised from the notated / edited edition published in the anthology, and that editing-and-compilation work is protected. The upstream author likewise states that the scanned images are copyrighted and withheld
  • We therefore do not grant commercial rights: these tracks are placed in C3 Study — research, teaching and study only; commercial use and public performance are prohibited

Attribution required: credit "Anthology of Chinese Folk Songs (OMR dataset, github.com/m-july/Anthology-of-Chinese-Folk-Songs)". The catalog license field for these tracks is TRADITIONAL-STUDY. For commercial use, obtain permission from the underlying rights holders.

Data quality: the upstream notes the dataset is not fully cleaned and measure boundaries may need normalising; some lyric characters are missing in Volume Shaanxi II — use it with care.

If any rights holder objects, contact us via the project Issues; we will remove this batch within 7 business days.

Our Principles

  • Redistribution required — no permission, no inclusion
  • Tiered labeling — C1 / C2 / C3 written per track
  • Better safe than sorry — unclear or restrictive licenses are excluded regardless of scale
  • Auditable — every decision archived and reviewable
  • Takedown-ready — rights-holder notices answered within 7 business days
